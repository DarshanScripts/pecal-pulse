Service-fit discovery

For a selected customer, the review pane shows equipment groups calibrated by at least 25% of recent active peers in the same industry, but absent from that customer's recorded last-year activity. At least 20 peers are required; generic / unknown industries are excluded. This suggests a question to ask about equipment and service fit, not a proven unmet need.

An appropriate next step is to confirm the customer owns relevant equipment, then discuss whether calibration support is useful. Price, margin and intervention outcomes must be added before calling an opportunity financially valuable.

Are the probabilities believable?

Later test: mean predicted probability versus actual return fraction by score bin

A perfectly calibrated model follows the diagonal. The lowest-score bin still had observed returns; no score is a guarantee. Overlapping windows and repeated customers create correlated observations.

What adds predictive signal?

Validation permutation importance: decrease in ROC-AUC after shuffling a feature. This is global predictive evidence, not a causal explanation or an individual customer's reason for inactivity.

Performance across prediction dates

Each origin predicts the following three months. All outcomes are available by August 2026. These repeated origins are not five independent customer samples.

Exact volume is still uncertain

Target is the next three months' calibration record count including zeros, not order count. The selected baseline is retained because it beat the tested ML regressors on validation. Its individual-customer test error remains too large for firm commitments. Model-supported rows are shown as rough volume context; unsupported customers have no estimate.
