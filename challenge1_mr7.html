Data quality and joins

How the workload is calculated

Each service is matched to a historical calibration using instrument UUID and SAP order number. Pairs associated with more than one lab are excluded. Processing minutes are joined through DIENSTLEISTUNGEN.KATALOGNUMMER = ArtikelnummerZeit.Artikelnummer, then summed by the service completion week. Unmatched processing times contribute no known hours and remain a limitation.

How sparse history is handled

  • Repeated history contributes the previous interval and calibration count.
  • Single-history instruments borrow equipment-group patterns.
  • No-return tails are right-censored; missing future events are not labeled failures.
  • Thirty-day probabilities are calibrated on a later period before the test is evaluated.
  • Unknown or unsupported ages are shown as abstentions.
The export is a current snapshot. Historical equipment reassignment and incomplete customer retention information can still bias recurrence estimates.
